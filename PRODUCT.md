# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fashion-conscious adults (18–40) shopping for modern outerwear online. They discover on mobile, decide on desktop. They expect premium: fast, beautiful, effortless. They are not bargain hunters — they are buying identity.

## Product Purpose

RAIR Clothing is a direct-to-consumer outerwear brand selling crew-necks, hoodies, knitwear, and shirts. The site lets customers browse the full collection, add to cart, and check out. Admins manage inventory and orders. Success = a customer who lands on the home page and places an order in under 5 minutes.

## Brand Personality

Cold. Precise. Uncompromising.

## Anti-references

- Generic Shopify themes (beige, rounded cards, friendly sans)
- Streetwear/hype brands (bold gradients, loud typography, Supreme-style)
- Editorial magazine aesthetic (display serif + italic + broadsheet grid — overused AI lane)
- Gold-on-black luxury cliché (Rolex DNA — wrong brand register)

## Design Principles

1. **Silence is expensive.** Negative space is the primary luxury signal. Resist the urge to fill.
2. **One color does the work.** The electric accent is the only saturated surface. Everything else is black or near-black.
3. **Type as architecture.** The condensed display face IS the brand mark. Use it with conviction.
4. **Motion earns its keep.** Every animation has a reason. None are decorative.
5. **Product is the hero.** The UI disappears. The clothing fills the frame.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). Full keyboard navigation. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. `color-scheme: dark` declared. All interactive elements have visible focus rings.
